Who We Are

Built on a medical foundation since 1989.

BIOMED launched in 1989, set up with a strong medical background as the concrete base of the company. Over the years, the company grew rapidly and gained enormous experience — the ability to provide the right kind of service for the ever-increasing needs of professionals in the medical field. Our state-of-the-art technology, along with the latest infrastructure, facilitates a business of dependable, quality instruments for surgeons in their fields of surgery.

BIOMED has a fantastic marketing system — the team is ever ready to assist customers at every stage and to provide after-sales service. We aspire to offer quick, personalized service and innovative solutions to our customer base, and have built our reputation as a complete, comprehensive one-stop solution for the medical fraternity.

  • Stainless steel & titanium orthopaedic implants
  • Pre-sterilized with gamma irradiation on request
  • Comprehensive surgical instrument sets
  • Manufactured in India, exported worldwide

Our Support to Indian & International Partners

At BIOMED, we value human relationships the most — our team strives to offer and establish the best working partnerships. India is a vast country of over a billion people with vast cultural diversity, an environment that has to be taken into account when deciding on business in India.

Our principal objective is to forecast changes in the business environment.
